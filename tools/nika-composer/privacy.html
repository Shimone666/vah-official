Privacy Policy

Nika VAH · Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy applies to the public demo tools hosted under the Nika VAH / vah-official project, including Nika Composer.

What we collect

The public Nika Composer demo runs entirely in your browser. Lyrics, chords, and prompts you type are stored only in your browser session (or localStorage if you use save features). We do not operate a backend that collects this content.

Meta / Facebook / Instagram

If you connect a Meta app associated with Nika VAH, we only request permissions needed for the stated use case (for example publishing or managing content on pages/accounts you authorize). We do not sell personal data. You can revoke access in your Meta settings at any time.

Third parties

This static demo does not embed third-party analytics trackers. External links (GitHub, X) are subject to those services’ own policies.

Contact

Questions: via @Nika_vah on X, or the repository maintainer on GitHub.
