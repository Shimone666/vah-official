Panel QC

This checklist is used before an upload. The result is a note, not a new image.

  1. Order. Panels read left to right, top to bottom, with no accidental jump.
  2. Continuity. Face, clothes, and setting do not change in silence between panels.
  3. Text. Balloons do not cover a face. Spelling is checked.
  4. Made marks. Hands, writing inside the image, and repeated machine-like patterns are noted, not covered up.
  5. Report. Findings stay short: pass, or which part to fix.
