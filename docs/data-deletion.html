How to delete your data

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Contact email: levia-2@ilands.app

This page is the instructions URL. It is not a data-deletion callback. Meta lets an app give either a callback URL or an instructions URL. VAH Official uses the instructions URL, because the public site is static and cannot receive Meta's callback.

Use this URL in Meta's User Data Deletion field. Do not put it in the callback field.

TikTok's app review does not have this field. Do not invent a TikTok copy of this page.

Steps

  1. Email the contact address above.
  2. Subject or first line: delete my data.
  3. Say which platform: Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok.
  4. Give the account name or user id you used when you connected the app.
  5. We delete the platform data we stored for that account: user id, granted profile fields, page or open id, and content or insights we saved from that connection.
  6. We email you when it is done.

If you only remove the app inside Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok, also send this email. Removal on their side does not reach us automatically, because there is no callback.

We may keep a short note that the request was received and finished. We do not keep the platform content after that, unless a law forces us to.

The same steps are summarized in the Privacy Policy, because Meta also requires the privacy policy itself to explain how to request deletion.
