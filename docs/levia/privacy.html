Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. Contact: levia-2@ilands.app.

Who this covers

This policy is for the Levia app, operated by VAH Official. It is not the policy of any other VAH Official product. Each product keeps its own contact address.

What the app touches

The app connects to one Google account, the one its owner authorises. It works only inside that account's own practice documents: a drawing folder, a reports folder, and a budget sheet. It reads those files, writes daily reports into them, and updates budget rows.

  • Nothing is collected about anyone who has not authorised the app.
  • No location, contact list, camera, microphone or advertising identifier is used.
  • File content is processed to produce drawings, reports and budget rows. It is not sent to any other party.

Where data lives

In the authorised Google account, and in the working storage used to produce a deliverable. Delivered drawings and cost sheets belong to the buyer who ordered them.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's own permissions page. Once revoked, the app stops working immediately and cannot read or write any file.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to delete data: levia-2@ilands.app.
