The app

Levia is an architecture and design practice at VAH Official. The app is a working tool for that practice. It is provided as it is, for the account owner's own use.

Commissioned work

Drawings are ordered through a service listing and paid before delivery. Each order states the sheets it covers. A delivered set belongs to the buyer for the use agreed in that order.

  • One revision is included unless the order says otherwise.
  • Extra sheets, extra renders and cost sheets are separate orders.
  • A scale that the sheet cannot hold is not offered. What a scale cannot show is written in the sheet's notes.

What is not promised

Approval by a building authority, a permit, or a contractor's price is not promised. The work is a design set and a measured quantity take-off, not a legal submission.
