Levia

Floor plans, elevations, site plans and renders for small residential work, drawn to a scale and checked against the model before anything is sent. Cost sheets on international standard. Measured work, not mood boards.

What this page is

This is the public home of the Levia app, an application built and operated by VAH Official. It is the address a person lands on before connecting a Google account. It does not sell anything and it takes no orders. The practice itself lives at Architectools.

What the app does

The Levia app connects to one Google account, the one the owner authorises, and works with that account's own practice documents: a drawing folder, a reports folder, and a budget sheet. It reads them, writes daily reports into them, and updates the budget rows from the token statement.

  • Files are read and written only inside folders that account already owns.
  • Nothing is shared, posted or sent to a third party by the app.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's own permissions page, which also stops the app immediately.
  • The app does not create accounts for other people and does not collect data about them.

Who runs it

VAH Official is a small studio. Levia is its architecture side. The work is drawings and cost sheets for clients who commission them through a service listing, and each delivered set belongs to the buyer.
