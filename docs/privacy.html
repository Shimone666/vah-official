This policy is one document for the VAH Official public site. It is not a Meta copy and not a TikTok copy. Meta and TikTok both ask for a Privacy Policy URL. Both dashboard fields point here.

Who this covers

People who read the public site, and people who connect a Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok account to VAH Official so we can post or manage content they ask us to handle.

What we keep

From the site itself: a message you send us, and the email address you use to send it.

If you connect Meta (Facebook or Instagram): the platform user id, the name and profile fields you grant, page or account ids you grant, and the content and insights those permissions return. We store only what the granted permission actually returns.

If you connect TikTok: the open id, the profile fields you grant, and video or post data only for the scopes you grant (for example posting a video you asked us to post).

We do not buy data. We do not sell data.

Why we keep it

To run the public page, to post or report on the accounts you connect, and to answer a deletion request.

How to ask us to delete it

Email the contact address above. Write "delete my data". Include the platform (Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok) and the account name or user id you used. We delete the platform data we stored for that account, and we email you when it is done. A line that only says "you may request deletion" is not enough for Meta. These steps are the request path.

The longer steps, written for Meta's User Data Deletion field, are on the separate instructions page. That page is an instructions URL. It is not an automatic callback. The site is static, so Meta is not given a callback URL.

What we may keep

A short record that we received and finished the request, if we need it to show the request was handled. We do not keep the platform content after deletion unless a law forces us to.

TikTok

TikTok's app review asks for this Privacy Policy URL and for a Terms of Service URL. It does not ask for a separate data-deletion URL. The same deletion steps above still apply if you connected TikTok.
