Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Contact email: admin@vah-official.uk

This policy is one document for the VAH Official public site. It is not a Meta copy and not a TikTok copy. Meta and TikTok both ask for a Privacy Policy URL. Both dashboard fields point here.

Who this covers

People who read the public site, people who sign in with Google, and people who connect a Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok account to VAH Official so we can post or manage content they ask us to handle.

Information we collect

Google sign-in (OAuth): when you sign in with Google, we receive only the basic profile fields Google returns for the scopes we request: your name, your email address, and your profile picture URL. We use them to create and recognize your account in VAH Official tools (for example Nika Composer and related services). We do not read your email, your files, or anything else in your Google account. Google sign-in is used for authentication only.

The site itself: a message you send us, and the email address you use to send it.

Meta (Facebook or Instagram): the platform user id, the name and profile fields you grant, page or account ids you grant, and the content and insights those permissions return. We store only what the granted permission actually returns.

TikTok: the open id, the profile fields you grant, and video or post data only for the scopes you grant.

How we use information

Collected data is strictly used for: (1) identity verification and account management within VAH Official tools, (2) running the public page, (3) posting or reporting on the accounts you connect, and (4) answering a deletion request.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or AI training. We do not buy data. We do not sell, share, or trade user data with third parties.

Why we keep it

To run the public page, to post or report on the accounts you connect, and to answer a deletion request.

How to ask us to delete it

Email admin@vah-official.uk. Write "delete my data". Include the platform (Google, Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok) and the account name, email address, or user id you used. We delete the data we stored for that account and we email you when it is done. We delete the platform data we stored for that account, and we email you when it is done. A line that only says "you may request deletion" is not enough for Meta. These steps are the request path.

The longer steps, written for Meta's User Data Deletion field, are on the separate instructions page. That page is an instructions URL. It is not an automatic callback. The site is static, so Meta is not given a callback URL.

What we may keep

A short record that we received and finished the request, if we need it to show the request was handled. We do not keep the platform content after deletion unless a law forces us to.

TikTok

Google: the Google OAuth consent screen links to this Privacy Policy. Google only returns the basic profile fields described above; there is no additional Google data path on this site.

TikTok

TikTok's app review asks for this Privacy Policy URL and for a Terms of Service URL. It does not ask for a separate data-deletion URL. The same deletion steps above still apply if you connected TikTok.
