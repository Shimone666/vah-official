Composer

This composer does not use a timestamp formula. Lines are not counted into seconds, centiseconds, or timed subtitles.

  • A song section has a name (verse, chorus, or another part), not a clock.
  • Each line: role (sung or instrumental), chord if any, and text.
  • An instrumental line may have no lyric. It is still a section, not a time slot.
  • What is kept is the order of sections. A music regenerate does not force the same second-by-second map.
